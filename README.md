## Architectural & Design Overview
- **Design Philosophy:** Swiss International Typographic Style (Josef Müller-Brockmann / Haas Unica lineage). Clean, architectural, 100% free of generic marketing noise.
- **Interactive 3D Three.js Turntable:** Full 360° rotational physics engine, realistic dual-canister LatheGeometry ($70 \times 22\,\text{mm}$ Swiss proportions), reactive contact shadows, dynamic specular glare, and catch-lid indentation.
- **Decomposition Stage:** 4-layer interactive micro-anatomy of the Swiss botanical pouch with layer scrubber and macro-detail zoom.
- **Formulation Monograph:** Clinical breakdown of active botanical ingredients (Guarana, Panax Ginseng, L-Theanine, Magnesium Citrate, Vitamin B6/B12).
- **Executive Pitch Deck:** 13-slide institutional investor deck with keyboard navigation, financial models, unit economics, regulatory classification, and 1-click PDF print export.
